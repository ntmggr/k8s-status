✓OKHealthy and matching git.
✕DEGRADEDGenuinely broken, needs attention.
!WARNINGArgoCD cannot determine health.
↻PROGRESSINGRolling out. Not an error.
≠DRIFTPods healthy, config differs from git.
⊘PRUNEOrphan awaiting cleanup, not an outage.
⏸SUSPENDEDDeliberately paused.

{{$.Snapshot.AcceleratorBadge}} waitingWants {{$.Snapshot.AcceleratorLabel}} and has not got any: none free, or no node to run on.

{{icon "mesh"}}Cluster-wide Istio mTLS only. Mesh-wide policy only: namespace and workload PeerAuthentications can override this for individual services and are not read.
✓STRICT mTLSEvery connection in the mesh must be mTLS-encrypted; plaintext is rejected.
!PERMISSIVE mTLSIstio's own default: accepts both mTLS and plaintext, so an unencrypted connection still gets through.
✕mTLS disabledPlaintext only; nothing in the mesh enforces encryption.
⏸No service mesh detectedIstio is not installed. A legitimate architecture choice, not a fault.
HEALTHThe gauge's needle and number are the share of counted services that are OK right now. Prune and suspended rows are excluded from the count; progressing and drift still count as not-OK. Shows — when nothing is left to count.
az 2Running pods sit in this many availability zones right now.
az 1All running pods sit in one zone: that zone going down takes the whole service with it.
ZONEDShare of zone-eligible services that spread across more than one zone right now.
MULTI-NODEShare of node-eligible services that spread across more than one node right now, tracked independently of zone spread.
IN MESHShare of mesh-eligible services carrying the Istio sidecar on every running pod right now. Sidecar injection only, not a per-service mTLS enforcement guarantee. An Istio ingress gateway reads as "in mesh" too, but that's its own Envoy engine, not an injected sidecar -- gateways are never sidecar-injected by design.
Status is the workload’s health; Sync is whether its manifests match git , two independent things, so PROGRESSING beside Synced is normal. Rows are ordered worst-first. Expand a row to see which components are out of sync. Click a tile to filter the list to that status, and click it again to clear it. A red or yellow stripe on the left edge of a row means DEGRADED or WARNING: the two states worth actually looking at. PROGRESSING and DRIFT get no stripe -- neither needs action right now, even though Health only counts PROGRESSING as healthy, since drifted config does not resolve on its own the way a rollout does. {{if $.MultiSource}}
Flux rows show their controller in the Source column. Flux does not compare running manifests against git the way ArgoCD does, so those rows carry no Sync value and never appear as DRIFT or PRUNE.{{end}}
